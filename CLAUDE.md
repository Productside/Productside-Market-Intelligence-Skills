# AI Working Contract — Market Intelligence Skills

[`CONSTITUTION.md`](CONSTITUTION.md) overrides everything in this file. Read it first.

## What This Project Is

Twenty-two instructional skills teaching market and competitive intelligence as collection disciplines, for Product Managers, Product Marketers, and Business Analysts. Distilled from the `market-intelligence` directory of `github.com/deanpeters/product-manager-prompts`.

It is a **network, not a chain**: five stages — instantiate, collect, fuse, act, monitor — and a user can legitimately enter at any of them.

## Before Changing Anything Under `skills/`

Read [`docs/SKILL-SPEC.md`](docs/SKILL-SPEC.md). It is the authoring and behavioral source of truth: the frontmatter contract, the thirteen required sections in order, the stage doctrine each skill must state, and the standard for worked and weak examples.

Then run:

```bash
./scripts/test-library.sh
```

Validation is substantive, not cosmetic. It checks the frontmatter schema, the portable projection, section order, the three evidence labels, the do-not-invent list, the Final Step block, the stage's doctrine phrase, at least five Key Concepts each carrying a `*Violation signal:*` line, and the required structure of both examples.

## The Registry Is Authoritative

`SKILL_REGISTRY` in `scripts/validate-skills.py` maps every skill to its stage and the collection discipline it speaks for. A directory not in the registry fails validation; a registry entry with no directory fails too. Adding a skill means editing the registry first.

Validation also fails if any of the seven collection disciplines loses its `collect`-stage skill, because `reference/sweep-playbooks.md` names seven channels and a channel with no runnable skill is a door that does not open.

## Writing Rules

- **ASCII only, no emojis** in files under `skills/` and `reference/`.
- **Prose, not diagrams, under `skills/` and `prompts/`.** Those are read by an agent at inference time, where a diagram costs tokens restating the Workflow section and renders as raw source in most viewers. Diagrams belong in `README.md` and `reference/`, and every mermaid node label must be quoted — an unquoted label can produce `](`, which the link checker reads as a broken relative link.
- **Never flatten canonical frontmatter by hand**, and never commit `agents/openai.yaml`. Both are generated by the packaging pipeline from the canonical source.
- **Do not blend the vocabularies.** "Collection is not fusion" is doctrine, not a slogan; keep collect-stage and fuse-stage language distinct.

## Examples Are Load-Bearing

The weak examples are where most of the teaching lives, and they are the hardest thing in this Project to write well. An obviously bad example teaches nothing, because nobody believes they would produce it.

A weak example must be work a competent team would actually ship — correct schema, real citations, disciplined vocabulary, populated fields — with a flaw of *degree and judgment* underneath: six sources that collapse to two disciplines, an announcement graded as commitment, a monitor reporting a copy tweak as material change. Then it explains why a reviewer would miss it.

Every example is synthetic, says so at the top, and uses `example.invalid` for URLs.

## Language

Productside is a services firm. Describe this Project's contents as **digital takeaways and examples that demonstrate and extend Productside's teaching and advisory services**. Avoid: code, codebase, software, application, script, technical project files. Prefer **Project** over "repo" where it reads naturally.

## When You Are Unsure

Prefer the honest gap to the plausible sentence. That preference is the whole method, and it applies to how this Project is built as much as to what it teaches.
