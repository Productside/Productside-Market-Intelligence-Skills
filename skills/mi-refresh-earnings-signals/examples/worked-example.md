# Worked Example: The Phrase That Ran Four Quarters and Stopped

**Synthetic teaching case.** Meridian Revenue Systems is fictional, and every quote, metric, segment name, and call date below is invented for teaching. Nothing here is a claim about any real company or executive, and no real person is quoted.

## Run Header

~~~
As-of date:        2026-08-18
Prior profile:     meridian-earnings-refresh-2026-05-20.md
Quarters compared: Q3 FY2026 vs Q2 FY2026
Language window:   5 consecutive quarters of transcripts held (Q3 FY2025 - Q3 FY2026)
Sources:           Q3 FY2026 call transcript (2026-11-04), 10-K FY2026 (2026-02-18),
                   Q2 and Q1 FY2026 transcripts, investor day deck (2026-04)
Stored as:         meridian-earnings-refresh-2026-08-18.md
~~~

**Everything in this run sits at Announced on the commitment ladder.**

## 1. Shifted Signals

| Topic | Was (Q2 FY2026) | Now (Q3 FY2026) | Read |
|---|---|---|---|
| Growth framing | "balanced growth across all segments" | "disciplined growth in our highest-conviction markets" | Inference: from breadth to selection. "Highest-conviction" is a phrase used when something is being deprioritized and not named. |
| Public sector | mentioned twice, in passing | mentioned nine times, including in opening remarks | Inference: promoted. Corroborates the reporting-structure change below. |

## 2. Dropped Language

| Phrase | Consecutive quarters present | Last appeared | Status now | Read |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **"land and expand"** | **4** (Q4 FY2025 - Q2 FY2026) | Q2 FY2026 | **Absent entirely** — not in prepared remarks, not in Q&A | Inference: the expansion motion has been retired or renamed. Four consecutive quarters and then nothing is a decision already made internally. |
| "seat-based growth" | 3 | Q2 FY2026 | **Demoted** — absent from prepared remarks, appeared once in Q&A in response to a direct question | Inference: no longer a headline story, but not abandoned. A demotion, not a disappearance. |
| "mid-market momentum" | 2 | Q1 FY2026 | Absent | Two quarters is thin. Logged, not read. |

The first row is the run. "Land and expand" ran four consecutive quarters, appeared in the investor day deck in April, and is completely absent from the November call — including from Q&A, where an analyst asked about net revenue retention and the answer used entirely different vocabulary.

Nothing prompted this finding. It required the prior profile's phrase list open beside the current transcript, searching for absence. Nobody notices a phrase that is not there.

The third row shows the discipline working in the other direction: "mid-market momentum" is also absent, and two quarters is not enough to call it dropped. It is logged for next quarter.

## 3. Metric Selection

| Metric | Prior treatment | Current treatment | What it displaced | Read |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Net revenue retention | Led with, every quarter for 4 quarters | Not mentioned in prepared remarks; given only when asked | — | Inference: it stopped being flattering. Consistent with the "land and expand" disappearance — both describe the same motion. |
| "Committed annual value" | Absent | Introduced in opening remarks, described as "the measure that best reflects the durability of our business" | **Net revenue retention** | Inference: a new metric introduced as "the better measure" is nearly always replacing one that got worse. |

The pairing is what makes this readable. A new metric alone is a curiosity; a new metric introduced in the same quarter that its predecessor stops being mentioned is a substitution, and the phrase justifying it is doing the work.

## 4. Reporting Structure

| Change | Detail | Source | Read |
|---|---|---|---|
| Public Sector split out from Enterprise | New reporting segment, first time | 10-K FY2026, 2026-02-18 | Inference: management intends to be measured on it. A segment gets its own line when it is being prioritized, not when it is being harvested. |

## 5. Deflection Log

| Analyst question | Response | Deflected in prior quarters? | Read |
|---|---|---|---|
| Public-sector gross margin | Two analysts asked; both received answers about "long-term margin profile" without a current figure | **Yes — Q2 FY2026, same topic, same framing** | Second consecutive quarter deflected. This is now a topic they have decided not to discuss, and the most likely reason is that the promoted segment is being won on price. |
| Net revenue retention | Answered when asked, but with a shift to committed annual value mid-answer | First time it required asking | The redirect is the signal, not the answer |

The margin question deflected twice in a row is the second-most-valuable line in this document. A single deflection is a busy executive; two in the same framing is a prepared position.

No annual filing fell in this window. The FY2026 10-K diff was performed in the prior run and is not repeated here.

## So What

- **Most consequential shift:** "land and expand" disappearing, net revenue retention going unmentioned, and a replacement metric arriving — three signals of one story, which is that the expansion motion is not working. This touches our positioning brief, which currently characterizes Meridian as expansion-led.
- **Commitment level:** **Announced.** All of it is language. What would confirm it: deferred revenue trends and segment margin disclosure in the next filing — `mi-collect-finint` finds both. Nothing here should be rated above a working hypothesis on its own.

## Assumptions to Validate

1. **"Land and expand" disappearing is read as a retired motion.** It could be a deliberate vocabulary refresh with the same strategy underneath. The net revenue retention demotion argues against that, but the two could share a communications cause rather than a strategic one.
2. **The margin deflection is read as price-based winning.** It could equally be a mix effect they do not want to explain quarterly.
3. **The language window is five quarters.** A phrase that ran ten quarters and stopped would be a stronger signal than one that ran four, and we cannot see beyond five.
