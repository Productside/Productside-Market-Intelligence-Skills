# Roadmap

Directional only. Nothing here is a commitment to build, maintain, or deliver.

## Under Consideration

**Additional regional overlays.** `reference/regional-overlays.md` covers EU and MENA. APAC, LATAM, and Sub-Saharan Africa would follow the same pattern: procurement platforms, company registries, statistics bureaus, standards and regulatory databases, plus the region-specific guardrails. Each overlay is a research project, not a template fill.

**A win/loss skill.** The HUMINT sweep ends by generating interview questions and raising a gap flag, because public signals infer why deals move and only interviews know. The interview synthesis itself is currently out of scope, and every build-signal story in this library is capped because of it.

**A positioning-statement handoff.** `reference/frameworks.md` describes the say-versus-said-about gap as positioning raw material and hands it off. There is no skill here that writes the statement.

**Worked examples using a single continuous case.** Several examples already share the fictional Cartelane engagement. Extending that consistently across all twenty-two would let a reader follow one investigation from routing through monitoring.

## Deliberately Out of Scope

**Anything that collects for you.** These are instructional materials describing a method. They are not a data service, and the Project stores no competitive research about any real company.

**A utility tier.** Precedents Thinking caps its supporting utilities at six so the method stays findable. This Project takes the stricter position: every skill collects for a named discipline, fuses named disciplines, consumes fused evidence into a named artifact, or diffs a named prior run. Nothing else earns a place. See the contribution test in `CONTRIBUTING.md`.

**Paid-source integrations.** The reference material names paid tools where they exist, because knowing what you are not buying is useful. Building against them is not.
